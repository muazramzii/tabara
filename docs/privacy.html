Privacy Policy

Tabara — personal finance tracker · Last updated 16 August 2026

Who this is about

Tabara is a mobile app for tracking personal spending and savings, built and operated by Muaz Ramzi. This policy explains what the app collects, why, where it is kept, and how you can get it removed.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not create an account.

What Tabara collects

Account information

  • Email address — used to identify your account and to send password-reset codes.
  • Password — stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider. Nobody, including us, can read your password.
  • Display name — the name the app greets you by. You choose it and can change it at any time.

If you sign in with Google or Facebook instead, we receive your name, email address and account identifier from that provider. We do not receive your password, your friends or contacts, your posts, or anything else from those accounts.

Financial information you enter

  • Transactions: amount, whether it is income or an expense, category, date, and any note you add.
  • Your monthly income figure, savings goal, and per-category budget limits.

These are figures you type in yourself. Tabara has no connection to any bank, e-wallet or payment provider.

Stored on your device only

  • Your login session, so you stay signed in.
  • A marker recording the highest logging streak you have celebrated, so the animation does not repeat.

This information never leaves your phone and is erased when you uninstall the app.

What Tabara does not collect

  • Bank logins, card numbers, or any payment credentials.
  • Your location.
  • Your contacts, photos library, messages, or call history.
  • Advertising identifiers. Tabara contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Tabara asks for camera and photo access only at the moment you choose to scan a receipt, and uses it for nothing else.

How the AI features work

Tabara includes a chat assistant (“Kapy”) and a receipt scanner. Both are powered by Google’s Gemini API.

Receipt scanning

When you photograph a receipt, the image is sent to Google’s Gemini API to read the total, merchant name and date. The image is not stored by Tabara — it is used for that single request and discarded. Only the values you then confirm and save are kept.

Chat with Kapy

Your messages, together with a summary of your current budget figures, are sent to Google’s Gemini API to generate a reply. Conversations are not stored on our servers — they exist only in the app while the screen is open and are gone when you leave it.

Your name, email address and account identifier are never sent to Google as part of these requests. Data sent to the Gemini API is handled under Google’s Gemini API terms.

The AI is used to read receipts and answer questions. It is never used to decide what you are allowed to see. Every request is checked against your own account first, so the assistant can only ever work with your data.

Where your data is kept

Tabara uses Supabase for its database and authentication. Your data is stored on servers located in Tokyo, Japan (AWS ap-northeast-1).

If you are in Malaysia, this means your personal data is transferred outside Malaysia. By using Tabara you consent to that transfer, as required under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Every database table enforces row-level security tied to your account identifier, so one user cannot read another user’s records.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual transactions yourself from the History screen at any time, and that removal is immediate and permanent.

To delete your entire account and everything in it, email muazramzi.123@gmail.com from the address you registered with. Your account, profile and all transactions will be permanently erased within 30 days, and we will confirm when it is done. Deletion cannot be undone.

Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia), you may:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and request a copy.
  • Correct anything that is wrong — most of it you can edit directly in the app.
  • Withdraw consent and have your data deleted.
  • Limit how your data is processed.

To exercise any of these, email muazramzi.123@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Children

Tabara is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated. Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect. Continuing to use Tabara after a change means you accept the updated policy.
